About

Plan-a-Shpiel is a desktop app that helps directors and production teams plan a show before it reaches the stage. It was built for Yiddish theatre, with full right-to-left support, so scripts, scene names and props can be written in Yiddish or Hebrew as naturally as in English.

What it does

  • Import a script and break it into scenes.
  • See the set from above in the stage plan view, and from the seats in the audience view.
  • Block the action: place characters and move them through each scene.
  • Keep track of props for every scene.
  • Save projects as files on your own computer, and export your blocking as a PDF.
