NoteBeast

About

NoteBeast is a cloud-hosted music library and band session app. It keeps your songs and music sheets organized and backed up in the cloud, and lets a whole band follow the same sheet together, whether you're online or offline.

What it does

  • Organize your music in folders and subfolders, with tags, genres and notes on every song.
  • Search for any song and read its lyrics, right inside the app.
  • Build playlists and keep your whole library synced and backed up across your devices.
  • Download music sheets so they're ready offline, on stage or in the rehearsal room.
  • Share sheets with bandmates in real time.
  • Host synchronized band sessions, so everyone follows the same song at the same moment.
