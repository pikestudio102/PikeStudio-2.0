NoteBeast

About

NoteBeast is a mobile app built for working musicians. It keeps your songs, lyrics and sheets in one place, works offline when you're on stage or in a rehearsal room, and lets a whole band follow along together.

What it does

  • Search for songs and read their lyrics, with a paragraph mode that focuses on one section at a time.
  • Run a live session over local Wi-Fi so every musician sees the same song during rehearsal.
  • Keep your library available offline.
  • Unlock extra features with a NoteBeast+ subscription.
